The planes around your home, on a small ESP32 board with a screen. Every plane says where it is on an open radio frequency. People all over the world catch it with small antennas and share it. Your board asks for the planes near you and draws them live.

Plug the board in with a USB cable. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  1. 1 CONNECT
  2. 2 YOUR BOARD
  3. 3 FLASH
  4. 4 LIVE
